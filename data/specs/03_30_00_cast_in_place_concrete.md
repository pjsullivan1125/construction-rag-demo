# SECTION 03 30 00 - CAST-IN-PLACE CONCRETE
*Fictional specification excerpt for the "Lakeside Medical Pavilion" training project. Not real project data.*

## PART 1 - GENERAL

### 1.1 SUMMARY
A. This Section covers cast-in-place concrete for footings, foundation walls, slabs on grade, and elevated slabs.

### 1.2 SUBMITTALS
A. Mix designs: Submit a mix design for each class of concrete at least 14 days before the first placement.
B. Product data: Submit data for admixtures, curing compounds, and vapor retarder.
C. Shop drawings: Submit reinforcing steel shop drawings showing bar sizes, spacing, and splices.
D. Test reports: Distribute cylinder compressive-strength test reports within 48 hours of each test.

### 1.3 QUALITY ASSURANCE
A. The Owner's testing agency will cast one set of four cylinders for each 50 cubic yards of concrete, or fraction thereof, placed in a day.
B. Cylinders are tested as follows: one at 7 days, two at 28 days, and one held in reserve.

## PART 2 - PRODUCTS

### 2.1 CONCRETE MIXTURES
A. Minimum 28-day compressive strength: footings and foundation walls, 4,000 psi; slabs on grade, 4,500 psi; elevated slabs, 5,000 psi.
B. Maximum water-cement ratio for all classes: 0.45.
C. Slump: 4 inches, plus or minus 1 inch, before addition of any superplasticizer.
D. Air entrainment: 5 to 7 percent for concrete exposed to weather.

### 2.2 REINFORCEMENT
A. Deformed bars: ASTM A615, Grade 60.
B. Welded wire reinforcement: ASTM A1064.

### 2.3 VAPOR RETARDER
A. Provide a vapor retarder of at least 15 mil thickness, complying with ASTM E1745, Class A, under all slabs on grade.

## PART 3 - EXECUTION

### 3.1 PLACEMENT
A. Place concrete within 90 minutes of batching.
B. Cold weather: When air temperature is below 40 degrees F, provide protection and a cold weather plan. Do not place concrete on frozen subgrade.

### 3.2 CURING
A. Keep concrete moist for a minimum of 7 days.
B. Curing compound may be used except at surfaces that will receive adhesive-applied floor coverings.

### 3.3 FORM REMOVAL
A. Vertical forms may be removed after 24 hours with the Engineer's approval.
B. Shores under elevated slabs remain in place until the concrete reaches 75 percent of its specified strength.
