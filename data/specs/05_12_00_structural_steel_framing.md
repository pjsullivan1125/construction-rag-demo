# SECTION 05 12 00 - STRUCTURAL STEEL FRAMING
*Fictional specification excerpt for the "Lakeside Medical Pavilion" training project. Not real project data.*

## PART 1 - GENERAL

### 1.1 SUMMARY
A. This Section covers structural steel framing, base plates, and connections.

### 1.2 SUBMITTALS
A. Shop drawings: Submit shop drawings and erection drawings showing member sizes, connections, and welds.
B. Mill certificates: Submit mill certificates for all structural shapes.
C. Bolt certifications: Submit certifications for high-strength bolts.
D. Welding procedure specifications (WPS): Submit for all shop and field welding.
E. Fabricator qualifications: The fabricator shall hold AISC certification for steel building structures.

## PART 2 - PRODUCTS

### 2.1 MATERIALS
A. Wide-flange shapes (W-shapes): ASTM A992, Grade 50.
B. Hollow structural sections (HSS): ASTM A500, Grade C.
C. Plates: ASTM A36.
D. High-strength bolts: ASTM F3125, Grade A325, except Grade A490 where noted on the drawings.
E. Anchor rods: ASTM F1554, Grade 36.

### 2.2 FABRICATION
A. Fabricate in accordance with AISC 360.
B. Shop-apply one coat of primer, except at surfaces to be welded or fireproofed.

## PART 3 - EXECUTION

### 3.1 ERECTION AND INSPECTION
A. Erect members plumb and level within the tolerances of the AISC Code of Standard Practice.
B. The testing agency shall inspect all field welds visually. Complete-joint-penetration welds receive 100 percent ultrasonic testing.
C. Verify bolt pretension by the turn-of-nut method.
