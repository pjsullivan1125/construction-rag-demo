# SECTION 07 92 00 - JOINT SEALANTS
*Fictional specification excerpt for the "Lakeside Medical Pavilion" training project. Not real project data.*

## PART 1 - GENERAL

### 1.1 SUMMARY
A. This Section covers exterior and interior joint sealants and related backing materials.

### 1.2 SUBMITTALS
A. Product data: Submit manufacturer's product data for each sealant.
B. Color charts: Submit color charts for the Architect's selection.
C. Test reports: Submit compatibility and adhesion test reports from the manufacturer for each substrate, including concrete, metal, and glazing.
D. Mockup: Submit a mockup of a sealant joint at least 12 inches long.

### 1.3 WARRANTY
A. Manufacturer's warranty: 5 years against adhesive and cohesive failure.
B. Installer's warranty: 2 years.

## PART 2 - PRODUCTS

### 2.1 SEALANTS
A. Exterior joints: silicone sealant, ASTM C920, Type S, Grade NS, Class 50.
B. Interior joints: acrylic latex sealant, ASTM C834.
C. Backer rod: closed-cell polyethylene.

## PART 3 - EXECUTION

### 3.1 INSTALLATION
A. Joint width-to-depth ratio shall be 2:1 for movement joints.
B. Install sealants only when the temperature is between 40 and 100 degrees F.
C. Tool sealant within 20 minutes of application.

### 3.2 FIELD QUALITY CONTROL
A. Perform field adhesion testing in accordance with ASTM C1193, Method A, at a rate of one test per 1,000 linear feet of joint.
