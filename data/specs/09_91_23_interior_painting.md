# SECTION 09 91 23 - INTERIOR PAINTING
*Fictional specification excerpt for the "Lakeside Medical Pavilion" training project. Not real project data.*

## PART 1 - GENERAL

### 1.1 SUMMARY
A. This Section covers surface preparation and painting of interior walls, ceilings, doors, and trim.

### 1.2 SUBMITTALS
A. Product data: Submit manufacturer's product data and VOC content for each paint.
B. Color samples: Submit 8-by-10-inch brush-outs of each color and finish for the Architect's approval.
C. Maintenance data: Submit touch-up and cleaning instructions.

### 1.3 MOCKUPS
A. Paint one patient room and one corridor section for each finish for approval before starting production work.

## PART 2 - PRODUCTS

### 2.1 PAINT
A. Primer: interior latex primer.
B. Finishes: flat at ceilings, eggshell at walls, semi-gloss at doors and trim.
C. VOC content shall not exceed 50 grams per liter.

### 2.2 COLORS
A. The Architect will select colors from the manufacturer's full range, with up to three colors per room.

## PART 3 - EXECUTION

### 3.1 PREPARATION
A. Surface temperature shall be at least 50 degrees F during application.

### 3.2 APPLICATION
A. Apply one coat of primer and two finish coats.

### 3.3 CLEANING
A. Remove paint spatter from adjacent surfaces at the end of each day.
